Food Ordering App — UI/UX Case Study

A streamlined food ordering experience designed to reduce friction, improve clarity, and speed up daily meal ordering.

RoleUI/UX Designer
PlatformMobile App
Duration4 Weeks
ToolsFigma

Problem

Users often face unnecessary complexity during login, menu browsing, and checkout, leading to confusion and abandoned orders.

Goal

Design a fast, intuitive ordering flow that keeps users informed and confident at every step.

Process

Defined core user flows, built mobile-first wireframes, and refined the interface through usability-focused iterations.

Solution

A clean, structured UI with transparent pricing, delivery estimation, and minimal decision friction.

Reflection

This project emphasized clarity, trust, and how small UX details significantly affect user confidence.
